## Evidence promotion rule

To change a scientific claim to `supported`, the evidence entry must bind:

1. preregistration commit and amendment history;
2. exact model, inference, runtime, grader, task-group and price-table hashes;
3. every independent search-run closure, including failures;
4. one atomic sealed authorization over the signed committed-edit registry and
   final champions, with a signed aggregate release;
5. prespecified co-primary reliability, sealed-utility, NI, and multiplicity
   outputs, plus the independently preregistered simultaneous one-sided lower
   bounds for any `>10 pp` headline family;
6. complete solver/proposer/diagnoser/grader usage;
7. a reproducible figure/table source and an explicit limitation statement.

Any opened sealed item, model revision drift, missing condition, post-hoc
endpoint change, asymmetric rerun, post-seal sample-size continuation, or
outcome-driven method iteration marks that lineage invalid rather than silently
downgrading it to a favorable complete-case analysis.  A changed method starts
a new preregistration with an independently committed sealed set.
