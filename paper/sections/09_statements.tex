\subsection*{AI use statement}

Generative AI tools were used in this work for hypothesis and idea refinement,
experimental-design feedback, implementation support, literature organization,
translation, manuscript drafting and editing, artifact
generation, and production of the mechanism schematic.  They have not been
used to generate, alter, or fill in confirmatory observations; that study has
not yet been completed.  This source is intentionally marked as a working
draft: before submission, the human authors must complete source-by-source
literature and plagiarism checks, review and test AI-assisted code, recompute
the prespecified analyses from immutable aggregates, inspect every table and
figure, and resolve every evidence-blocked marker.  No AI-generated rationale
is treated as empirical evidence.  The authors remain responsible for the
final text, claims, code, and artifacts, including all AI-assisted material.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}

Self-modifying harnesses can amplify unsafe instructions, poisoning, privilege
escalation, secret leakage, deceptive evaluator behavior, and excessive
resource use.  Our design limits editable surfaces, denies candidates grader
and sealed-set authority, records provenance and usage, tests protected
behavior, and retains an exact parent for rollback.  These controls are not a
security proof; deployment with tools or sensitive data requires process
isolation, least privilege, network and secret controls, independent audit, and
human authority over release.  Benchmark calls also consume energy and shared
model-serving resources, so we report failures and all role-specific usage
rather than only successful solver calls.  The planned study uses benchmark
and generated tasks and does not involve human subjects or private user data.

record are \evidenceblocked{not yet frozen and independently reproduced}.
