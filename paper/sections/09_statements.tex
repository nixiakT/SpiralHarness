\subsection*{AI use statement}

Generative AI tools assisted with hypothesis and idea refinement,
experimental-design feedback, conceptual and statistical formulation,
implementation, tests and artifacts, literature organization and translation,
interpretation of development pilots and method-development diagnostics,
manuscript drafting and editing, scientific figures, and compliance and
red-team audits.  They were not used to generate, alter, or impute confirmatory
observations, and no absent experiment or result is filled from model output;
the confirmatory study has not been completed.  No generative-AI output is
treated as an observation, ground truth, source validation, or empirical
evidence.

Human authors remain responsible for the final text, claims, code, and
artifacts.  Automated tests and AI-assisted audits do not substitute for human
verification.  Before submission, the authors must inspect every retained
AI-assisted change, complete source and plagiarism checks, review and test the
code, recompute prespecified analyses from immutable aggregates, and inspect
every table, figure, and supplement artifact.  This working draft does not
represent any incomplete human check as complete.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}

Self-modifying harnesses can amplify unsafe instructions, poisoning, privilege
escalation, secret leakage, deceptive evaluator behavior, and excessive
resource use.  The present implementation provides typed, in-process limits on
editable surfaces, provenance, usage, and exact-parent rollback; it does not yet
provide the target out-of-process denial of grader and sealed-set authority or a
hostile-code sandbox.  These controls are not a security proof; deployment with tools or sensitive data requires process
isolation, least privilege, network and secret controls, independent audit, and
human authority over release.  Benchmark calls also consume energy and shared
model-serving resources, so we report failures and all role-specific usage
rather than only successful solver calls.  The planned study uses benchmark
and generated tasks and does not involve human subjects or private user data.
