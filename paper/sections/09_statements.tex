\subsection*{AI use statement}

Generative AI tools were used in this work for hypothesis and idea refinement,
experimental-design feedback, implementation support, literature organization,
translation, interpretation of development pilots and method-development
diagnostics, conceptual and statistical formulation, synthetic benchmark and
fixture design, manuscript drafting and editing, code and artifact generation,
and production of scientific figures and schematics.  They have not been used
to generate, alter, or fill in confirmatory observations; that study has not
been completed.
No AI-generated rationale is treated as empirical evidence.  Human authors
remain responsible for source and plagiarism checks, review and testing of
AI-assisted code, recomputation of prespecified analyses from immutable
aggregates, inspection of every table and figure, and the final text, claims,
code, and artifacts.  The submission-readiness checklist records these checks;
this working draft does not represent an unchecked item as complete.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}

Self-modifying harnesses can amplify unsafe instructions, poisoning, privilege
escalation, secret leakage, deceptive evaluator behavior, and excessive
resource use.  Our design limits editable surfaces, denies candidates grader
and sealed-set authority, records provenance and usage, tests protected
behavior, and retains an exact parent for rollback.  These controls are not a
security proof; deployment with tools or sensitive data requires process
isolation, least privilege, network and secret controls, independent audit, and
human authority over release.  Benchmark calls also consume energy and shared
model-serving resources, so we report failures and all role-specific usage
rather than only successful solver calls.  The planned study uses benchmark
and generated tasks and does not involve human subjects or private user data.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

The supplement is intended to contain the frozen protocol, mutation schemas,
generator and design-matrix hashes, exact environment and model manifests,
complete search closures including failures, usage ledgers, signed aggregate
inputs, and deterministic analysis and figure sources.  The current repository
contains the typed verification and replay infrastructure and this figure's
pure TikZ source.  A clean, anonymous artifact and one-command reproduction
record are \evidenceblocked{not yet frozen and independently reproduced}.
Confirmatory claims will not be unblocked until those materials reproduce from
a clean checkout without access to sealed item-level targets.
