\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Without confirmation, all headline and confirmatory empirical claims remain
blocked.  Controlled faults simplify real failures, and probes may perturb
behavior, so inference covers only the frozen roster.  The in-process boundary
is not hostile-code isolation; deployment needs sandboxing and secret controls.
Rare commits can yield low false promotion; precision is undefined at zero,
and sealed utility only limits this tradeoff.  Gating adds cost.  FULL--SCORE
is joint; the factorial is component-level, not mediation.

The BFCL pilot uses public data and repeats the same eight HOLDOUT tasks under
three search seeds; its 24 cells are not independent task samples.  It covers
only a 15-task partial roster, not the official full BFCL suite, hidden data, or
sealed evidence.  A common nominal provider route and configuration also do
not attest an identical served model revision.  Its negative result is useful
development evidence, but not a confirmatory benchmark conclusion.
