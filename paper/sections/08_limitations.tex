\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Without confirmation, all empirical claims remain blocked.  Controlled faults
simplify real failures, and probes may perturb behavior, so inference covers
only the frozen roster.  The in-process boundary is not hostile-code isolation;
deployment needs sandboxing and secret controls.  Rare commits can yield low
false promotion; precision is undefined at zero, and sealed utility only limits
this tradeoff.  Gating adds cost.  FULL--SCORE is joint; the factorial is
component-level, not mediation.
