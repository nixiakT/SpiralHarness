\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

DSPy, GEPA, agent-design search, and STOP optimize prompts, programs, workflows,
or code \citep{khattab2023dspy,agrawal2025gepa,hu2025adas,zelikman2024stop}.
Meta-Harness, Agentic Harness Engineering, Self-Harness, and PenguinHarness move
this search to persistent harness state
\citep{lee2026metaharness,lin2026ahe,zhang2026selfharness,
prismshadow2026penguin}.  These systems establish that harness edits can be
generated; we study the narrower decision of when an adaptively selected edit
should be committed.  Accordingly, \systemname{} is positioned as a commit-
control layer behind an optimizer, not as a universally stronger search
algorithm.

The closest boundary is verification.  Harness Updating Is Not Harness Benefit
separates authorship from activation and adherence; HarnessBank already combines
activation, paired significance, and gain gates; HarnessFix and HarnessCompass
add localized, regression-aware or constrained evolution
\citep{lin2026updating,luo2026harnessbank,chen2026harnessfix,
zhang2026harnesscompass}.  HarnessOpt-Bench and Evo-Bench evaluate held-out
optimizer capability and transfer \citep{ursekar2026harnessopt,
huang2026evobench}.  Our unverified distinction is primary family-level false-
promotion control joined to sealed usefulness, with child--parent--revert--
placebo attribution and a feedback-by-promotion factorial.

SEAL motivates an external acceptor and PACE gives paired anytime-valid commit
tests \citep{guo2026seal,shawn2026pace}; we claim neither the first independent
nor sequentially valid gate.  Reusable-evaluation and confidence-sequence theory
also require their stated filtration assumptions
\citep{dwork2015reusable,howard2021confidence}.  Campaign spending and mechanism
evidence do not repair leakage, invalid graders, dependent families, or a mutable
authority.
