\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Optimization beyond model weights.}
DSPy compiles LM programs, GEPA evolves prompts from reflective feedback,
automated agent design searches workflows, and STOP improves code-level
artifacts \citep{khattab2023dspy,agrawal2025gepa,hu2025adas,zelikman2024stop}.
We study promotion evidence after such adaptive optimization.

\paragraph{Harness optimization and self-evolution.}
Meta-Harness exposes source, traces, and scores to a coding-agent proposer;
Agentic Harness Engineering emphasizes observability; and Self-Harness performs
weakness mining and regression-gated updates
\citep{lee2026metaharness,lin2026ahe,zhang2026selfharness}.  PenguinHarness
demonstrates persistent instruction editing in a recursive public example
\citep{prismshadow2026penguin}.  These systems rule out ``first self-evolving
harness'' claims.  Meta-Harness asks what program an outer agent discovers;
our factorial asks whether feedback or promotion authority changes policy
outcomes.

Harness Updating Is Not Harness Benefit already distinguishes authorship from
activation and adherence, and HarnessBank combines activation,
paired-significance, and positive-gain gates
\citep{lin2026updating,luo2026harnessbank}.  HarnessOpt-Bench evaluates held-out
normalized gain in a trusted environment, while Evo-Bench fixes a policy model
and evaluates a long-horizon evolver
\citep{ursekar2026harnessopt,huang2026evobench}.  They measure optimizer
capability and generalization rather than identifying which promotion evidence
caused an update to survive.  At our audit cutoff neither had a public
executable release, so both remain paper-only comparators unless that changes
before the experiment freeze.

HarnessFix uses trace-grounded localization and scoped, regression-aware
repair, while HarnessCompass combines constrained evolution, proactive agent
feedback, and component-wise optimization
\citep{chen2026harnessfix,zhang2026harnesscompass}.  They narrow novelty in
diagnosis and guidance, but do not identify promotion-policy effects.

SEAL motivates an acceptance signal outside a self-rewriting verifier, while
RHO studies label-free retrospective signals \citep{guo2026seal,pan2026rho}.
Population, routing, decentralized composition, localization, prompt-level
recursion, and weight-updating variants broaden the search setting
\citep{liu2026adaptive,nie2026evolvenet,wang2026handbook,chen2026harnessx,
lee2026recursive,chen2026coharness,ning2026evoharness}.  Our unverified
distinction is intervention-based mechanism attribution, campaign false
promotion, and the feedback-by-promotion factorial.

\paragraph{Adaptive validity.}
Reusable evaluation can overfit held-out data \citep{dwork2015reusable}, and
confidence sequences support optional stopping only under their stated
filtration assumptions \citep{howard2021confidence}.  PACE directly casts each
paired commit decision as an anytime-valid test under optional stopping
\citep{shawn2026pace}; we therefore do not claim the first sequentially valid
acceptor.  We add mechanism evidence, full-campaign spending, and a factorial;
neither guarantee repairs leakage, invalid graders, dependent families, or a
mutable authority.
