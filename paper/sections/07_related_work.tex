\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Optimizing prompts, programs, and workflows.}
DSPy treats language-model pipelines as compilable programs
\citep{khattab2023dspy}; GEPA evolves prompts from reflective trajectory
feedback \citep{agrawal2025gepa}; and automated design of agentic systems
searches compositional workflows \citep{hu2025adas}.  STOP and related recursive
systems demonstrate that language models can improve code-level artifacts
\citep{zelikman2024stop}.  These works establish powerful optimizers.  Our focus
is the evidence required for promotion after adaptive selection, not a claim
that mutation itself is new.

\paragraph{Harness optimization and self-evolution.}
Meta-Harness gives a coding-agent proposer filesystem access to accumulated
source, traces, and scores and searches full harness code
\citep{lee2026metaharness}.  Agentic Harness Engineering emphasizes
observability-driven evolution \citep{lin2026ahe}.  Self-Harness mines
weaknesses, proposes bounded edits, and regression-tests changes using the same
model family that operates the harness \citep{zhang2026selfharness}.  These are
the closest conceptual baselines and rule out broad novelty claims such as
``first self-evolving harness'' or ``first evidence-based update.''  Our
budget-matched \fullarm/\scorearm{} contrast isolates whether intervention-linked
mechanism evidence changes promotion quality.

Harness Updating Is Not Harness Benefit explicitly separates the ability to
author an update from the solver's ability to activate and follow it
\citep{lin2026updating}; activation and adherence are therefore not novel
constructs here.  HarnessBank combines a separate evolver, semantic diversity
archive, and gated screening \citep{luo2026harnessbank}.  HarnessOpt-Bench
standardizes optimizer evaluation with fixed budgets, held-out normalized
gain, and a trusted execution environment \citep{ursekar2026harnessopt}.
\systemname{} instead treats promotion evidence as the intervention: it holds
the optimizer and search budget fixed between score-only and mechanism-visible
conditions, and requires child--revert--placebo attribution before benefit can
authorize an update.  This difference is empirical and remains
\evidenceblocked{the matched sealed contrast has not been run}.

SEAL shows that at least one acceptance signal must remain outside an agent
that rewrites its own verifier \citep{guo2026seal}; thus we do not claim the
first exogenous acceptor.  Our narrower question is what source-linked evidence
that acceptor should require under repeated adaptive nominations.  RHO removes
the need for labeled deployment feedback through retrospective
self-consistency and self-preference \citep{pan2026rho}.  That signal regime is
complementary, but our confirmatory study intentionally retains trusted task
outcomes so that false promotion and causal attribution are identifiable.

\paragraph{Adaptive validity.}
Reusable evaluation can overfit even when each query appears held out
\citep{dwork2015reusable}.  Confidence sequences provide time-uniform inference
under optional stopping when their assumptions and filtration are respected
\citep{howard2021confidence}.  \systemname{} applies this perspective at the
campaign level, but does not claim that a statistical wrapper repairs data
leakage, invalid graders, non-independent fault families, or a mutable
evaluation authority.
