\section{Prospective Evaluation Design}
\label{sec:experiments}

The confirmatory study asks one question: does \fullarm{} make adaptive commit
decisions that are both \emph{credible} and \emph{useful}?  Credibility is
measured by family-level false promotion on controlled faults; usefulness is
measured once on untouched real-task data.  The answer is positive only when
both primary families and all non-inferiority gates pass.  The current design is
\evidenceblocked{pilot-pending: margins delegated below, the roster, replication
count, analysis, and sealed authority are not frozen}.

Within each model--task--search-seed block, \scorearm{} and \fullarm{} must share the
seed harness, exact model revision, role settings, mutation grammar, task and
rollout coordinates, nomination rule, query topology, and hard call/token
ceilings.  The trusted plane must execute the same mechanism probes and blinded
judge calls in both arms and charge them to both; SCORE receives only the frozen
performance projection, while its mechanism outputs remain feedback-free shadow
measurements.  Thus evidence collection and information disclosure are not
confounded with extra calls.  The real-task contrast therefore estimates the joint deployable
policy, not feedback alone.  A controlled-fault \(2\times2\) crosses score or
mechanism-visible feedback with performance-only or mechanism-gated promotion;
component effects and a shuffled-evidence placebo are secondary.  Exact arm
projections, matching rules, and the factorial are specified in
Appendix~\ref{app:evaluation-details}.

\purearm{} is an effectiveness reference with the same solver revision and
task coordinates, not a budget-matched search policy.  \(\textsc{Pure@B}\)
will separately spend FULL's ex-ante call/token ceiling on independent
provider-minimal samples under a task-native aggregation rule frozen without
grader or sealed feedback.  Same-model self-evolution requires one exact
provider-resolved revision for every model-mediated role and no manual or
stronger-model candidate choice; otherwise the run is a heterogeneous harness-
optimization baseline.

The prospective fixed roster contains
\designvalue{three open model configurations from at least two families} and
\designvalue{four source-separated task families spanning mathematical,
heterogeneous-reasoning, classification, and structured-generation behavior}.
One complete search run is the repeated algorithmic unit; rollouts inside a run
are not independent replications.

\subsection{Co-primary decision rule}

For the frozen model--task roster, let binary-success cell means define
\begin{align}
\Delta_{\mathrm{policy}}
  &= \operatorname{avg}_{m,t}\{\mu(\text{\fullarm},m,t)
       -\mu(\text{\scorearm},m,t)\},\\
\Delta_{\mathrm{e2e}}
  &= \operatorname{avg}_{m,t}\{\mu(\text{\fullarm},m,t)
       -\mu(\text{\staticarm},m,t)\}.
\end{align}
Cells receive equal weight.  The two co-primary families are:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Commit reliability.}  On independent null/unrepairable fault
  families, the one-sided simultaneous upper bound for
  \(p_{\mathrm{FP}}(\text{\fullarm})\) must be below 0.05, and the paired
  FULL--SCORE reduction must exceed a positive
  \designvalue{operationally frozen false-promotion margin \(\delta_{\mathrm{FP}}\)}.
  Both tests use one-sided Holm control within this family
  \citep{holm1979simple}.
  \item \textbf{Sealed usefulness.}  One-sided Holm tests must establish
  \(\Delta_{\mathrm{policy}}>0.02\) and
  \(\Delta_{\mathrm{e2e}}>0.02\) on the prospectively binary scale.
\end{enumerate}
The headline ``credible adaptive commit'' is the intersection of both families
and simultaneous protected-behavior and cost non-inferiority, plus provenance,
leakage, and missingness closure.  This is an intersection--union decision: low
false promotion cannot compensate for absent sealed benefit, and high utility
cannot compensate for harmful commits.  Verified-repair improvement, harmful-
promotion probability, accepted-edit precision, and component effects are key
secondary endpoints with their own frozen multiplicity families.  Zero accepted
edits makes precision undefined and cannot satisfy sealed usefulness.

Any \(>10\)-percentage-point statement is a separate, independently
preregistered headline family.  Before search, it must enumerate every contrast,
model, task, and baseline it will name and power the corresponding simultaneous
one-sided bounds.  It passes only if every multiplicity-adjusted lower bound is
strictly above 0.10 on the binary-success scale.  It cannot be added after the
sealed audit, borrowed from the co-primary tests, or used for post-seal sample-
size continuation or method iteration.

Every benefit, false-promotion, repair, protection, and cost margin must be
justified from a signed deployment utility/risk budget before the outcome-
bearing pilot; the pilot estimates nuisance variation and power, not a favorable
effect threshold.  The current nested-bootstrap analyzer and prospective simulator are
method-development prototypes: they predate this reliability family, lack
coverage calibration and authority-attested inputs, and cannot freeze the
sample size or support a claim.  The formal estimator, family dependence,
failure mapping, and all margins are frozen before the disjoint pilot and
confirmatory outcomes (Appendix~\ref{app:power-proxy}).

\subsection{One-time sealing and reporting}

All search cells must close before the target sealed authority receives the
signed registry of committed edits and final champions.  One authorization must evaluate the entire
registry under the same utility, protected-slice, cost, and missingness rules
used at the gate, and releases aggregates only.  A post-unseal failure ends that
lineage; changing the method requires a new preregistration and independently
committed sealed set.

We retain failures and report utility, protected outcomes, calls, uncached and
cached tokens, normalized and billed cost, wall time, retries, and score per
million tokens.  Exact retry, failure-as-zero, missingness, external-baseline,
and resource rules are in Appendix~\ref{app:evaluation-details}.
